## Form Fields

### 1. Email Address *

**Field Type:** Email Input  
**Required:** Yes

**Description:**  
Enter a valid email address for all official communications. A copy of your submission will be sent here.

**Note:** Your Google Account name, email, and photo will be recorded.

---

### 2. Team Name *

**Field Type:** Text Input  
**Required:** Yes

**Description:**  
Enter your team name exactly as registered on Devfolio. Must match character-by-character to avoid disqualification.

**Important:**  
- Case-sensitive matching required
- Must match your Devfolio profile exactly

---

### 3. Team Leader's Name *

**Field Type:** Text Input  
**Required:** Yes

**Description:**  
Enter the team leader's full name. This person will be the primary contact and receive all official communications.

**Responsibilities:**
- Primary contact for organizers
- Coordinates with team members
- Receives shortlisting updates

---

### 4. Total Members *

**Field Type:** Multiple Choice (Radio Buttons)  
**Required:** Yes  
**Options:** 2, 3, or 4

**Description:**  
Select your team size (2-4 members). Solo participation not allowed.

**Team Size:**
- Minimum: 2 members
- Maximum: 4 members
- Cannot be changed after submission

---

### 5. Upload your Idea Presentation (PDF) *

**Field Type:** File Upload  
**Required:** Yes  
**Accepted Format:** PDF only  
**Maximum File Size:** 10 MB

**Description:**  
Upload your project idea presentation using the official template. PDF format only, max 10 MB.

**Required Sections:**
- Problem Statement
- Proposed Solution
- Technology Stack
- Innovation & Impact
- Team Details
- Timeline

**Tips:**
- 8-12 slides recommended
- Use visuals and diagrams
- Focus on clarity and feasibility
